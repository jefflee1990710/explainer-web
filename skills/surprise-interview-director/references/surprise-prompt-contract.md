# Surprise Interview Prompt Contract (Phase B)

Write one video prompt per approved clip. Do not start until Phase A is approved.

Each prompt:

1. Duration in this clip's own seconds, aspect ratio, and the one character speaking that line to the lens.
2. First frame = this clip's start still. Last frame = this clip's end still.
3. Clip 1: the person stays right-side up. The camera starts above and drops downward while it snaps a zoom-in. Sharp and clear. Do not flip the picture. The face moves into surprise.
4. Clip 2: the seated interview begins. Camera locked.
5. Clip 3 and after: the first frame is the previous clip's last frame. Mouth, a nod, and at most one hand gesture. Camera locked.
6. On-canvas type is the shock poster: ultra-bold condensed sans, off-white words, mustard-yellow numbers, the payoff in black on a tilted yellow dry-brush stroke. Clip 1 keeps it huge at the top. Later clips keep the same design smaller at the bottom for the whole clip. Camera stays locked and does not zoom out. Letters stay upright. No subtitle bar. The spoken sentence is spelled exactly.
7. No background music. No extra writing.
