# Ending Proposal Contract (Phase A)

Present a readable director's proposal and stop for confirmation before writing the model prompt.

## Header contract

1. Title in the reference language and in English
2. Duration (3–4s), clip count 1, loop mode: always Linear
3. Sign-off / CTA and the resolve device
4. Aspect ratio, canvas, logo placement and size on the final card
5. Spoken line (or none) and the resolve SFX
6. Logo lock: attached logo exactly, or title lettering of the brand name

## Storyboard contract

| Clip # & time | Ending sting | Start still | Resolve move & settle | VO | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Exactly ONE row.

## Scene detail

Write `startScene` and `endScene` as four concrete parts, in order:

1. Logo: where it is, how big, and how complete (partial / converging / full card).
2. Set: canvas and the one or two simple shapes, props, or mascot pose around it.
3. Light: direction and mood; identical in start and end.
4. Camera: shot size and framing; identical in start and end unless the move is a zoom-out.

## Motion contract (`motionCamera`)

One timed move that fits 3–4 seconds, e.g. `0–3s the props slide inward and fold into the logo; 3–4s the logo settles at centre`. Every difference between the start and end still must be caused on camera in that move. No cuts.

## Phase A checks

- Exactly one clip, 3–4 seconds.
- The end still is the final logo card, readable and at rest.
- With a logo attached, nothing redraws or invents the logo.
- No technical colour notation.
