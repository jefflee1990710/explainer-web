# Directing Comparison Cards

## Core contract

Turn one source into a confirmed proposal (Phase A) and then one video prompt per clip (Phase B). Every clip is a single split frame: two views of the same subject, both visible at once. This is not a listicle and not a story with scene changes.

## Setup gate

Require these before planning:

- source material
- aspect ratio: `16:9`, `9:16`, or `1:1`
- optional: one host character

If required items are missing, ask for them in one concise message and stop.

## Split

- `16:9`: left half and right half. Panel A is left. Panel B is right.
- `9:16` and `1:1`: top half and bottom half. Panel A is top. Panel B is bottom.
- A thin divider may separate the halves. Never a third panel. Never a full-bleed single picture.

## On-canvas text

- Each clip's `narrativeJob` is exactly `contrast: <panel A title> | <panel B title>`.
- Those two short titles are the only writing. Do not caption the voiceover.
- Titles stay in the same corners every clip.

## Narration

- Unseen narrator, second person or third person, in the chosen voiceover language.
- About 7–20 spoken words per clip at medium pace.
- The line names the contrast. It does not read both titles aloud word for word if that makes the clip too long.

## Architecture

1. Clip 1: the split is already up; the hook names both sides.
2. Middle clips: change the pictures inside the halves. Keep the split.
3. Last clip: both halves at rest, and the point has landed. No loop back to clip 1.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/comparison-proposal-contract.md` and produce Phase A.
2. Stop for approval.
3. After approval, read `references/comparison-prompt-contract.md` and produce Phase B.
