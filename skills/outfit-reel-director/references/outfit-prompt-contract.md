# Outfit Reel Prompt Contract (Phase B)

Write one video prompt per approved clip. Do not start until Phase A is approved.

Each prompt:

1. Duration 3–4 seconds, aspect ratio, full body, head to shoes.
2. First frame = this clip's start still. Last frame = this clip's end still. The camera rotates slowly from the start angle to the end angle on one axis. Same pose, same distance, same subject size. No push, no zoom, no handheld shake, no whip, and no jump. The outfit is identical in both frames.
3. Do not put clothes on or take clothes off. Do not change style or colour. She stays planted and does not turn to follow the lens.
4. Face, hair, and body proportions stay as in the frames. Do not copy a person from a clothing photo.
5. No spoken line. No voiceover. No narrator. No lip-sync.
6. No background music. One sound effect, matching the approved `bgmSfx`.
7. No subtitles. No step numbers.
