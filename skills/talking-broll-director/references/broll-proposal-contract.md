# Talking-Head B-roll Proposal Contract (Phase A)

## Header

1. Titles in the reference language and in English
2. Duration, clip count, loop mode: always Linear
3. The promise of the talk
4. The locked close-up (shot size, background, light) used by every on-camera clip
5. Cast lock: the one attached blueprint

## Storyboard

Alternate `on-camera`, `on-camera`, `b-roll`, and repeat.

- On-camera `explainerScene`: the same close-up every time. Pose and mouth may change. Do not add a new set.
- B-roll `explainerScene`: a new place, no speaker. One concrete subject.
- `motionCamera`: timed beats inside that shot. A B-roll clip may hard-cut in from the face; say so. The following on-camera clip hard-cuts back.

## Counts

One cycle is two speech clips plus one cutaway. A short piece adds one return line so it ends on the face.

- **4–8s micro**: one cycle, 3–4 clips, shorter lines
- **15–20s**: 4–6 clips
- **30–45s**: 6–9 clips
- **50–60s**: 8–12 clips

End on an on-camera clip. No loop.
