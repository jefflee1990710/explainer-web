# Surprise Interview Prompt Contract (Phase B)

Write one video prompt per approved clip. Do not start until Phase A is approved.

Each prompt:

1. Duration in this clip's own seconds, aspect ratio, and the one character speaking that line to the lens.
2. First frame = this clip's start still. Last frame = this clip's end still.
3. Clip 1: the person stays right-side up. The camera starts above and drops downward while it snaps a zoom-in. Sharp and clear. Do not flip the picture. The face moves into surprise.
4. Clip 2 and after: hard cut. This clip's camera angle and body pose are different from the previous clip. Within the clip they stay locked. Only the mouth and expression move.
5. On-canvas type is the shock poster: ultra-bold condensed sans, off-white words, mustard-yellow numbers, the payoff in black on a tilted yellow dry-brush stroke. Pin it for the whole clip at that clip's place: top, middle, or bottom. Neighbouring clips do not share the place. Letters stay upright. No subtitle bar. The spoken sentence is spelled exactly.
6. No background music. No extra writing.
